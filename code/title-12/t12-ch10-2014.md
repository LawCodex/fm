# FSM Code 2014, Title 12, CHAPTER 10 Criminal Extradition [REPEALED in its entirety by PL 5-22 § 1]

**Editor's note:** PL 5-22, section 1 repealed the former chapter 10, "Criminal Extradition," in its entirety. PL 5-22 also enacted new chapters 14 on Criminal Extradition and 15 on Criminal Extradition Procedures of this title in its place.
