# FSM Code 2014, Title 36, CHAPTER 6 Credit Unions [RESERVED]

**Case annotations:** A financial institution, such as a credit union, that holds money from depositors does have an on-going fiduciary duty to its depositors. *Wakuk v. Kosrae Island Credit Union*, 7 FSM R. 195, 197 (Kos. S. Ct. Tr. 1995).
